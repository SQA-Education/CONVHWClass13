1. Conversation Awareness

Customer Service and Consumer Rights

Customer service is an important part of American business culture. Companies often try to resolve problems quickly to keep customers satisfied.

Customers are usually expected to explain their problem clearly and politely when requesting a solution.

Many companies offer refunds, replacements, or exchanges when a product is defective.
